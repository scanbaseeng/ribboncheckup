People arrive at this site looking for several different things under the name Ribbon Checkup. Some want to log in to an account. Some want to cancel or change a subscription. Some want customer service. Some want to understand a test result. This page sorts them out so nobody has to guess.

## Two sites, one name

**ribboncheckup.org**, the site you are on, is a health education publication. It is published by Ribbon Health Press, a Scanbase, Inc. company. It explains tests, lab numbers, and preventive screenings in plain language. It does not sell anything, has no accounts, and stores no personal data. Every page is free to read.

**ribboncheckup.com** is the product site for Ribbon Checkup home urine tests. That is where the tests are sold, where accounts and subscriptions live, and where customer support is handled. Scanbase operates both sites.

The two share a name because they share an owner. They are kept separate on purpose. Editorial content on this site is written to the same standard whether or not Scanbase sells a product in the category. Where a piece discusses a category of test that Scanbase sells, we say so on the page. Read the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for the full statement.

## If you want to log in

Go to **app.ribboncheckup.com**. That is the Ribbon Checkup app and account portal. This site cannot log you in and does not have access to any account.

## If you want to cancel, pause, or change a subscription

Go to **support.ribboncheckup.com**. Subscription changes, including cancellation, are handled through the account and support channels on the .com side. Nothing on this site can change a subscription, and we do not receive those requests.

## If you want customer service

Go to **support.ribboncheckup.com**. Order questions, shipping, app problems, and billing all go there.

## If you want to understand a result

That is what this site is for. Start with these:

- [What a 10 parameter dipstick measures](/health-explained/what-a-10-parameter-dipstick-measures/), for the pads on a standard urine test strip
- [Kidney health urine tests](/health-explained/kidney-health-urine-tests/), for protein, microalbumin, and uACR
- [How to read urine test strip results](/guides/how-to-read-urine-test-strip-results/), for technique
- [How to use home health tests without fooling yourself](/articles/how-to-use-home-health-tests-without-fooling-yourself/), for what a positive or negative actually means

None of this is medical advice. A home test result is a reason to talk to a clinician, not a substitute for one. See the [medical disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## If you want to reach the editors

Corrections, questions about an article, and topic requests: editors@ribboncheckup.org. That address reaches the editorial team only. It cannot help with accounts, orders, or subscriptions. Those go to the support site above.

## Why keep them separate

A health site that hides who owns it is not worth reading. A product site that pretends to be neutral is not worth trusting. Keeping the two apart, and saying plainly which is which, is the only honest way to run both. If you ever find a page on this site that reads like an advertisement, tell the editors. That is a mistake and it will be fixed.
