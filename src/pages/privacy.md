Ribbon Checkup is a static website. It has no accounts, no comments, and no forms.

## What we collect

Nothing directly. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard server request data such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not have access to individual visitor records.

## Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising pixels. If that changes, this page will be updated first and the change will be dated.

## Fonts

Pages load a typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser makes a request to Google's servers to fetch it, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

## External links

Pages may link to guideline bodies, government sites, and other references. Those sites have their own policies.

Questions: editors@ribboncheckup.org
