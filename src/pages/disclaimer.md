The content on ribboncheckup.org is provided for general informational and educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and reading it does not create a clinician and patient relationship.

Home tests and lab numbers are screening tools. A result does not confirm or rule out any condition on its own. Results are affected by hydration, diet, medication, timing, storage, technique, and individual factors that a website cannot know. Always follow the instructions supplied with your specific test, and confirm results with a licensed healthcare professional.

If you have symptoms, if a result concerns you, or if you have a condition that requires monitoring, consult a clinician. Do not delay or avoid seeking care because of something you read here.

If you think you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.
